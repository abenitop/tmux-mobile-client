# Privacy Policy — Tmux-Max Mobile

**Effective date:** September 24, 2026

Tmux-Max Mobile ("the app") is an SSH terminal / tmux client for Android. This
policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

## Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the
developer or to any third party.

## Data you provide, and where it lives

- Connection details (hostname, port, username, password, tmux session name)
  are entered by you and stored **only on your device**.
- Passwords are encrypted using the Android Keystore and never leave your
  device.
- The app connects **only** to the servers you configure, directly over SSH. No
  traffic is routed through any server or intermediary operated by the
  developer.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — required to connect to your server over SSH. The app requests
  no other permissions.

## Analytics, advertising, tracking

None. The app has no analytics SDK, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDK, and
no third-party trackers.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from
anyone.
